Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Applies to the CardNest iPhone app, this website, and email support.

The short version

  • Your cards are stored only on your iPhone, encrypted. CardNest has no servers, accounts, or cloud sync, and we never receive your cards.
  • Scanning happens on your iPhone, offline. Bank-card photos are never kept; other cards can keep a small photo, encrypted with the card.
  • CardNest contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party data-collection code.
  • If you delete the app or lose your iPhone, your saved cards cannot be recovered — by you or by us.

1. Who we are

CardNest (“CardNest”, “we”, “us”) makes the CardNest iPhone app. You can contact us at ekka.caffle@gmail.com.

This policy covers three separate things, each handled differently: the app (sections 2–8), emails you send us (section 9), and this website (section 10).

2. What CardNest stores on your iPhone

CardNest saves only what you enter or confirm after a scan, and only after you tap Save Card:

  • Business cards: name, company, phone, email, website, and notes.
  • Loyalty and membership cards: nickname, organization, card or member number, expiration date (membership), and notes.
  • Bank cards: bank name, name on card, expiration month and year, bank support phone, and either the full card number or only its last four digits — your choice.
  • Card photos (optional): a small, reduced photo of a business, loyalty, or membership card, from a scan or chosen from your photo library, shown in your card list and the card’s details. Bank cards never have a photo.
  • Whether a card is a favorite, and when it was last changed.

CardNest has no field for PINs or card security codes (CVV/CVC) and never reads them from a scan. Please don’t type them into any field, including notes.

This information stays on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

3. How your cards are protected

  • Encryption. Saved cards are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in the app’s private storage. The encryption key is kept in the iOS Keychain, is available only on this iPhone, only while a device passcode is set, and is not synced to iCloud Keychain. The encrypted file also uses iOS’s strongest file protection, which keeps it unreadable while your iPhone is locked.
  • App Lock. App Lock (Settings › Face ID / Device Authentication) is off by default. When you turn it on, your cards open only after Face ID (or Touch ID) or your iPhone’s passcode, and turning it on or off also asks for them. The setting is stored inside the encrypted vault. CardNest doesn’t create its own password. CardNest requires a device passcode to be set, because the encryption key is only available while one is.
  • Background locking. When you leave CardNest, it clears your cards from the screen and the app switcher shows a “Locked” cover instead of your cards. With App Lock on, you need Face ID or your passcode to open them again. Unsaved edits are discarded when you leave the app.
  • Masked bank numbers. Full bank-card numbers are kept apart from the rest of your card data and are never shown in lists or used for search; lists show only the last four digits. Showing a full number requires Face ID or your passcode every time, even when App Lock is off, and it hides again after 30 seconds, when you leave the screen, or when the app goes to the background. CardNest has no copy, share, or payment features.

We work to protect your information, but no app or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Your iPhone’s own security — a strong passcode, current iOS updates, and not sharing your passcode — matters a great deal.

4. Camera and scanning

  • Scanning uses Apple’s on-device document camera and text and barcode recognition (VisionKit and Vision). It works offline; nothing is uploaded by CardNest.
  • Photos. For bank cards, the captured image is used only in memory to read the card, then discarded. For other cards, a small reduced copy of the front is kept as the card’s photo when you save it, encrypted inside the vault with the rest of the card; the full-size image is discarded. CardNest never writes card photos to your Photos library, iCloud, or unencrypted files, and a photo is deleted with its card. You can remove a photo at any time.
  • Choosing a photo: CardNest uses Apple’s photo picker, so it only receives the one photo you choose and doesn’t need access to your library.
  • Bank cards: only the recognized card number, name on card, expiration month/year, and (when clearly printed) bank name are passed to the form for your review. The rest of the recognized text is discarded on the device.
  • Other cards: recognized text and barcode contents are processed in the app’s memory to suggest field values. For business cards, text that doesn’t fit a field may be placed in Notes so you can review it.
  • Scan results are suggestions. Nothing is saved until you review the form and tap Save Card. Canceling a scan leaves the form unchanged.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — only when you tap a Scan button. You can turn it off any time in Settings › CardNest; you can still enter cards by hand.
  • Face ID — to show a full card number, to turn App Lock on or off, and, when App Lock is on, to unlock your cards. Your face data is handled entirely by iOS; CardNest never receives it.

CardNest doesn’t request access to your photos, contacts, location, microphone, notifications, or tracking.

6. What we don’t collect

The app doesn’t collect personal data for us. It has no account sign-up, analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting SDKs, or other third-party data-collection code, and it doesn’t make network requests of its own. We don’t sell or share data, because we don’t receive it.

7. Links and other apps

If you tap Call, Email, or Website on a saved business card, iOS opens the Phone, Mail, or Safari app (or another app you choose). What happens there is governed by that app and service, not by CardNest.

8. Deleting data, backups, and data loss

  • Delete a card: open it and tap Delete Card. Delete everything: Settings › Delete All Cards. Deleted cards can’t be restored.
  • Deleting the app removes its saved cards from your iPhone.
  • Backups: CardNest asks iOS to exclude its saved cards from iCloud and computer backups, and its encryption key is tied to this iPhone. Apple describes backup exclusion as guidance to iOS rather than an absolute guarantee, but any copy restored to another device couldn’t be decrypted there. In practice, your cards won’t move to a new iPhone.
  • You can lose access if you delete the app, lose or reset your iPhone, or remove your device passcode (which removes the encryption key). CardNest has no export or recovery feature, and we can’t recover your cards. Keep your physical cards and official records.

9. Email support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems you report. Our support email is provided by Google (Gmail), so Google also processes these messages under Google’s Privacy Policy.

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and then delete them, and we’ll delete your messages sooner if you ask.

Never email card details. Don’t send full card numbers, security codes (CVV), PINs, or unredacted card photos. We will never ask for them. If you send them by mistake, we’ll delete the message.

10. This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, trackers, scripts, or external fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don’t receive or use those logs. Copies of these pages bundled inside the app load from the app itself, without a network connection.

11. Apple

You download CardNest through Apple’s App Store, which Apple operates under its own privacy policy. If you’ve chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. CardNest is designed not to write card details to logs, and we use these reports only to fix problems.

12. Children

CardNest isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly receive information from them.

13. Your choices and rights

You control the card data in the app: you can view, change, or delete it at any time, and we never have a copy. For emails you’ve sent us, you can ask for a copy or for deletion at ekka.caffle@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights; we’ll honor them for the limited information we hold.

14. Changes

If CardNest’s handling of information changes, we’ll update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect, and describe significant changes in the app’s release notes.
