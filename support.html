Support

Help with scanning, saving, and protecting your cards in CardNest.

Never send card details. Don’t email or share full card numbers, security codes (CVV), PINs, or unredacted card photos — not even with us. CardNest support will never ask for them. Describe the problem instead, and cover the numbers in any screenshot.

Unlocking and locking

  • App Lock is off by default, so CardNest opens straight to your cards. CardNest requires a device passcode to be set on your iPhone.
  • To require Face ID or your passcode, turn on Settings › Face ID / Device Authentication and confirm. Turning it off also asks you to confirm.
  • With App Lock on, tap Unlock My Cards each time you open CardNest, and it locks again as soon as you leave it. To lock right away: Settings › Lock Now (available while App Lock is on).
  • Whenever you leave CardNest, unsaved edits are discarded, so tap Save Card before switching apps.

Scanning a card

  1. Tap +, choose a card type, then tap the Scan button at the top of the form.
  2. Lay the card flat on a plain, contrasting surface in even light. Let it fill the frame and tilt it slightly to avoid glare on the numbers.
  3. Tap the shutter if the camera doesn’t capture automatically, then tap Save in the camera screen.
Loyalty and membership cards

CardNest reads the organization name and the card or member number from the barcode or printed text. If the barcode is on the back, scan the front and then the back as a second page. A QR code that only contains a website link is not used as the card number.

Bank cards

CardNest reads the card number, name on card, expiration month/year, and the bank name when it’s clearly printed. It never reads security codes or PINs. Numbers must pass a standard check; if the number can’t be read reliably, the field is left empty and you can tap Scan Again or type it. If a card shows only the last four digits, only those are filled. A card that has already expired is flagged so you can check the date.

Business cards

CardNest fills the name, company, phone, email, and website when it’s confident. Job titles and other text it can’t place go into Notes for you to review. If a card has two phone numbers, both stay in Notes so you can choose.

The scan didn’t work
  • Clean the camera lens, add light, and avoid shadows and reflections.
  • Embossed (raised) numbers read best at a slight angle with light from the side.
  • Make sure only one card is in view.
  • You can always enter details by hand.

Card photos

Business, loyalty, and membership cards can show a photo in your card list. Scanning adds a small photo of the card’s front automatically, or tap Choose Photo in the form to pick one from your library. Tap Remove to delete it. Photos are kept encrypted with the card on this iPhone and deleted with the card. Bank cards never keep a photo.

Correcting details

Every scanned value is a suggestion. Check each field against your card and edit anything that’s wrong. If you scan again, CardNest asks before replacing anything you’ve already typed. To fix a saved card, open it and tap Edit Card.

Saving

Nothing is saved until you tap Save Card. Bank cards don’t need a nickname — they’re listed as “Bank name • 1234”, or “Card • 1234” without a bank name. For the card number, enter the full number or just the last four digits.

Searching and favorites

  • Tap the search field and type a name, company, email, phone number, note, or card number. Use the type buttons (Business, Loyalty, …) to narrow results, and Clear to start over.
  • For bank cards, search matches the last four digits only — full numbers are never searchable.
  • Open a card and tap the star to add it to Favorites.

Showing a full card number

  1. Open the bank card and tap Show next to the masked number.
  2. Confirm with Face ID or your passcode — CardNest asks every time.
  3. The number hides again after 30 seconds, when you tap Hide, when you leave the screen, or when the app goes to the background.

When editing, the saved number stays hidden until you tap Show. Tap Replace to enter a new number. Changing it to just the last four digits removes the saved full number after you confirm.

Camera and Face ID permissions

  • If scanning says camera access is off: open iPhone Settings › CardNest and turn on Camera.
  • To use Face ID: Settings › CardNest › Face ID must be on, and Face ID must be set up in Settings › Face ID & Passcode. Otherwise CardNest uses your passcode.
  • CardNest asks for no other permissions.

Deleting cards

  • One card: open it and tap Delete Card, then confirm.
  • All cards: Settings › Delete All Cards, then confirm.
  • Deleted cards can’t be recovered.

New iPhone, backups, and data loss

For your privacy, saved cards stay on the iPhone where you added them. They aren’t synced or included in backups, and they won’t appear on a new or restored iPhone. Deleting the app, resetting your iPhone, or turning off your device passcode permanently removes access to them. There is no export or recovery, so keep your physical cards and official records.
