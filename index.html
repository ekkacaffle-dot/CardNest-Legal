CardNest

Keep business, loyalty, membership, and bank cards together on your iPhone — private, offline, and protected by Face ID or your device passcode.

  • On your iPhone only. No account, no cloud sync, no ads, and no analytics.
  • Scan on the device. Card text and barcodes are read offline by Apple’s on-device frameworks. Bank-card photos are never kept; other cards can keep a small encrypted photo.
  • Lock it your way. Turn on App Lock to require Face ID or your passcode. Full bank-card numbers always stay masked until you confirm with Face ID or your passcode.
CardNest is not a wallet. It can’t make payments and doesn’t replace your physical cards. It’s a private place to keep card details for reference.
